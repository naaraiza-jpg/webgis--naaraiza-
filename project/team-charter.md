## Working Agreement

Schedule weekly meetings using a shared availability calendar. Each member will make meaningful GitHub commits at least once per week while actively developing, including code, data, or documentation contributions. Tasks will have a named owner and deadline, and members will report blockers before those deadlines.

Weekly meetings will be via zoom.

## Decision process

Routine decisions will use a simple majority vote. Architecture, database design, and update methods require a recommendation from the developers and database manager, supported by feasibility, reliability, and student needs. The team reviews major changes and documents its reasoning;If disagreement remains, we will test alternatives through a small prototype or seek instructor guidance.

## Disappearing Member Clause

If a member does not respond for 48 hours during an active task, Jennifer will contact them directly and request a response within another 24 hours. If they remain unavailable, the team will document the contact attempts and reassign urgent work. When the member returns, the team will agree on a recovery task and deadline. Continued nonresponse or repeated missed commitments will be reported to the instructor, with reasonable consideration for emergencies or previously communicated absences.
